Our Story

Akbar Bhai began this mission in 1984 with a simple belief: every Muslim deserves to find a life partner who shares their deen, values, and vision for a beautiful family life.

What started as a humble local service in Umarkhed has grown into one of the most respected matrimonial offices in the region — trusted by thousands of families across Maharashtra and beyond.

Every rishta we facilitate is treated with personal attention. We don't just match profiles — we understand families, listen to concerns, and guide with wisdom and Islamic ethics at every step.

Our Commitment

We personally meet or speak with every family. There are no hidden fees, no false promises — just honest, halal matchmaking done the right way.

Our Islamic Values

Halal Process Only
All interactions follow Islamic etiquette. No free mixing. Wali involvement is mandatory in all communications.

Molvi Consultation
We offer proper Islamic guidance on compatibility and marriage prerequisites, keeping Deen as the primary criterion.

No Dowry Pressure
We firmly stand against dowry practices and encourage simple, blessed Nikahs as per Sunnah.

Confidentiality Always
Profile details and photos are shared only with mutual consent. Your trust is our greatest responsibility.
